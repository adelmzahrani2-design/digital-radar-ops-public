DIGITAL-RADAR-OPS

DIGITAL-RADAR-OPS is a research and digital-intelligence system for acquiring publicly accessible research materials, preserving provenance, extracting evidence-backed claims, and supporting human review.

This public page exists to identify the project when accessing public sources through automated research tooling.

Contact

For questions related to automated access, User-Agent identification, or source handling, please use the project's public GitHub Issues page: github.com/adelmzahrani2-design/digital-radar-ops-public/issues

Operating Principles

  • Respect source terms, robots policies, and rate limits.
  • Preserve source provenance and attribution.
  • Minimize collection of personal data.
  • Do not bypass access controls.
  • Human review remains required before research results are accepted.
